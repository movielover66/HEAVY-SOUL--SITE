Shipping & Store Policy

At Heavy Soul, every t-shirt is handmade by us, and we're committed to getting it to you in a timely, safe and transparent way. This policy covers order processing, delivery timelines, charges, payments, returns, privacy, and how to reach us.

Order Processing Delivery Timelines Shipping Charges Order Tracking Cash on Delivery Failed Deliveries Address Accuracy Cancellation Delays Returns & Exchange Privacy Contact & Grievance Business Details

1. Order Processing Time

  • Every piece is handmade by us after your order is confirmed, so please allow 2–3 business days for your order to be dispatched.
  • Delivery timelines begin only once your order is dispatched, not from the date the order is placed.
  • Orders are not processed on Sundays or public holidays.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Once dispatched, your order typically reaches you within 7 days, depending on your location and courier availability.
  • We currently ship across India. Delivery timelines may vary slightly for remote areas.
  • Occasional delays of 1–2 days may occur due to courier constraints beyond our control.

3. Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping applies on all orders above ₹699.
  • Orders below this amount carry a flat shipping charge, clearly shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Shipping offers may change during sales or promotional periods.

4. Order Tracking

  • Once your order is dispatched, tracking details will be shared with you on WhatsApp.
  • You can reach out to us anytime for a status update on your order.

5. Cash on Delivery ("COD") Orders

  • Pay in full: pay the full order value upfront via UPI.
  • Cash on delivery: pay a small advance via UPI to confirm your order, then the remaining balance in cash when it's delivered.
  • A COD handling charge applies per item and is shown clearly at checkout before you pay.
  • COD orders are dispatched only after your payment of the advance is confirmed. After paying, share your UPI transaction ID (UTR) — this sends your order to us on WhatsApp for confirmation.

6. Failed or Unsuccessful Deliveries

  • If delivery fails due to reasons such as unavailability or an incorrect address, a second delivery attempt will be made once we confirm with you.
  • If a third delivery attempt is required, additional shipping charges may apply and will be borne by the customer.

7. Address Accuracy

  • Please make sure the shipping address and contact details you provide at checkout are accurate and complete.
  • We're not able to take responsibility for delivery failures caused by incorrect or incomplete address details, so do double-check before confirming your order.

8. Order Modification and Cancellation

  • Since every piece is made specifically for you, any request to modify or cancel an order must be made before it is dispatched.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled and no refund will be issued.
  • Need something urgently? Message us on WhatsApp before placing your order so we can let you know what's possible.

9. Delays and Exceptional Circumstances

Delivery timelines are estimates and may occasionally vary due to courier delays, weather, festive season rush, or other circumstances outside our control. If your order is delayed well beyond the estimated time, please reach out to us — we'll keep you posted and make sure you're taken care of.

10. Returns & Exchange

  • Every t-shirt is handmade by us specifically for you, so we're unable to offer a return or exchange for reasons like a change of mind, size preference, or similar once production has begun.
  • If you need to change your size, just message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of placing your order, and we'll update it for you before we start making your piece.
  • The only other exception is if we've made a mistake — for example, if the wrong size is sent to you. In that case, we'll happily exchange it for the correct one, free of cost.
  • To request an exchange for a wrong size sent by us, message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with your Order ID and a couple of photos of the item.

11. Privacy

We only collect the details needed to fulfil your order — name, phone number, email (optional) and delivery address. This is shared only with our delivery partners, purely to deliver your order, and is never sold to third parties. For any privacy questions or to request your data be deleted, reach out to us on WhatsApp.

12. Contact & Grievance Redressal

We consider it our responsibility to give every customer a fair and timely resolution. If you have any query, complaint, or concern, please reach us at:

  • Email: tukaisana88@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: for order updates, exchanges, or quick questions, message us with your Order ID.

We usually reply within 24 hours on business days — we're a small, handmade brand, and every message matters to us.
